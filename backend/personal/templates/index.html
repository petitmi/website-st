About Me

Yuchen Zhang

Welcome. This is some stuff about me.

欢迎您来。这是我的一些介绍。

I studied Statistics for my undergrad, and a few years into my career, I went back to pursue a Master’s in Data Science. In between, I took a year off to study electric guitar at Midi School. So far, I’ve spent the first three-fifths of my life in Xi’an, the next three-tenths in Beijing, and the remaining one-tenth in Vancouver.

我本科是统计学，工作几年后，又读了数据科学硕士。中间有一年脱产在迷笛学了电吉他。 截至2026，我人生的前3/5在西安，中间3/10在北京，余下的1/10在温哥华

Right after I graduated with my bachelor’s degree, it happened to be the big data era. My title shifted between Data Analyst and Data Product Manager, and I’ve worked both at a niche startup and at an internet giant with hundreds of millions of users. The constant through it all has been that I’ve stayed in the internet industry.

本科毕业后正好是大数据时代，我的title在数据分析师和数据产品经理之间转换，在小而美的创业公司和用户好几亿的巨头都待过，不变的是都在互联网行业。

I am now working in Vancouver as a Data Analyst.

现在在温哥华做数据分析师。

Some of my favorite movies are Persian Lessons and 1917. My favorite books include Love in the Time of Cholera, the Neapolitan Novels, and Hidden Valley Road.

我最喜欢的电影是《波斯语课》和《1917》。最喜欢的书有《霍乱时期的爱情》、《那不勒斯四部曲》，和《隐谷路》。

I used to enjoy songwriting a lot, writing songs day and night, mostly about love obsession and political frustration. Some of them I produced hard and released on platforms including Spotify; you can find them by searching “xmmz.”

以前很喜欢写歌，乱七八糟写了很多，题材大多是爱情的沉迷和政治的愤怒。其中有一些作品，我很认真制作了，然后发布到了包括spotify的各个平台，可以通过xmmz搜到我。
